No — and you can know this without trying a single arrangement. A standard chessboard has 32 white and 32 black squares. Two opposite corners are always the same color, so removing them leaves 30 squares of one color and 32 of the other. Every domino, no matter how placed, always covers exactly one white and one black square. Therefore 31 dominoes must cover 31 white and 31 black squares — but the mutilated board has 30 and 32. The tiling is impossible because of a simple color imbalance, not a complex geometric barrier.
